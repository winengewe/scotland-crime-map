Data & Methodology

Establishing Trust & Precision

Why This Exists: The Importance of Open Intelligence

In an era of information, safety should never be a mystery. The **Scotland Safety Finder** was built to democratize public safety data, empowering residents, house-hunters, and travelers with the same level of granular insight used by professional analysts. By transforming raw government spreadsheets into an interactive spatial radar, we provide a vital tool for personal security planning and informed community awareness.

1. Transparency of Sources

We pull data exclusively from verified channels:
  • Police Scotland (Open Data Portal)
  • Scottish Government (SIMD Statistical Data)
  • National Records of Scotland (2020/2024 Census)

2. Methodological Precision

We use **Per Capita Normalization**. Instead of just counting crimes, we measure incidents per 1,000 residents. This ensures a quiet village and a busy city center are compared fairly on the "Safety Scale."

3. Recency & "Live" Logic

Our **2026 Edition** doesn't just show history; it tracks active police appeals and reported incidents in near real-time, providing immediate situational awareness.

4. Validation & Feedback

Safety is subjective. If you find a data discrepancy, we encourage community reporting via our GitHub Issues portal to maintain 100% precision.

Comprehensive Feature Guide

01
Master Search Bar

Type any street, city, or postcode. Our system uses a dual-engine search (local DB + Postcodes.io) to instantly focus the map on your target interest area.

02
Analysis Period Selector

Toggle between **Historical Stats (2020-2025)** and our **Live 2026 Radar**. Historical data shows long-term trends, while Live shows what is happening right now.

03
View Mode Toggles

Switch to **Heatmap** to see high-density "hotspots" across Scotland, or **Markers** to see precise locations of safety data zones and incident reports.

04
Intelligence Profile (Drawer)

Click any pin to open a deep-dive drawer. It shows: **Safety Rank** (National comparison), **Yearly Incidents** (Volume), and a **Local Feed** of recent nearby events.

05
Real-Time Geolocation

Click **"Locate Me"** to sync the map with your current physical position. This allows you to perform an instant safety audit of your immediate surroundings.

06
Category Filters

Filter the entire map by specific crime types like **Violence, Dishonesty, or Antisocial Behaviour** to customize the intelligence to your specific concerns.

Understanding the Safety Grade

A+ (Elite Safe) Top 5% Safest in Scotland
F (High Activity) Bottom 20% - Frequent Reporting

This site is for informational purposes only. Data is based on reported incidents and census statistics. Actual safety can vary by specific street conditions, lighting, and time of day. Always maintain situational awareness.
